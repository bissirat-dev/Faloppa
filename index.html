Travel to Capri

It is finally show-time in Capri, where the weather, the food, and the people are luxurious.

Mostly, it is very reasonable to travel here from London and the local hospitality services are incredibly accommodating.

I am celebrating what I have accomplished with a dream trip to see the stunning cliffs and the deep blue Mediterranean Sea while eating my favorite Italian dishes:

Foods you must eat in Capri:

  • Pizza Napoletana
  • Lasagna al forno
  • Pasta with shrimp sauce
  • Tiramisu

Whether you choose to spend your days hiking or enjoying fresh seafood by the water at Marina Grande, Capri delivers an unforgettable experience that stays with you long after you return home.
